Current task plan and Gantt​

Updated October 13, 2026. Beta: November 3–6, 2026. Pre-final defense: November 21, 2026. Both dates are fixed. The live board has the interactive Kanban and Gantt views, card movement, task links, and named change history.

This plan expands the 26 old aggregate issues into 56 individually owned cards. The old cards will move to Archive after the split; each group’s first small card keeps its existing issue link, and each additional card gets its own linked issue. Dates are inherited planning windows, not estimates of person-hours. Availability remains to be confirmed. See the role guide for boundaries and the parallel execution plan for safe work packets and handoffs.
